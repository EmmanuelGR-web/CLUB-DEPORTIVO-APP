# User Manual — Club Atlético San Martín System

## What is this application?

It's a platform where club members can, from their phone or computer:

- Check their membership status (active, years as a member, Gold/Silver/Bronze category)
- Pay their membership fee
- See past payments and pending ones
- Update their personal information
- View or download their digital membership card
- Check club information: fixtures, team roster, history, museum, etc.

## How do I sign up?

1. Open the application from your phone or computer.
2. Tap "Create account".
3. Fill in your information (name, email, password, etc.).
4. Done: your membership number is generated automatically, no need
   to visit the club's office in person.

## How do I pay my membership fee?

1. Log in with your email and password.
2. Go to the "Membership Fee" section.
3. Choose your preferred payment method: cash (at the club), bank
   transfer, debit, or credit card.
4. Confirm the payment. You'll be able to see the receipt in your history.

## How do I update my information?

1. Log in.
2. Go to "My Account".
3. Edit the fields you need (phone, address, etc.).

**Important**: your membership number (ID) can never be changed — it's
your permanent identifier at the club.

## What are the Gold/Silver/Bronze categories?

It's a recognition of your years as a club member:

- **Bronze**: up to 4 years as a member
- **Silver**: between 5 and 14 years as a member
- **Gold**: 15 years or more as a member

The category updates automatically — you don't need to do anything.

## What information can't I see?

For security reasons, the club's general financial movements (income,
expenses, etc.) are restricted to the club's main administrator only.
You can always see your own payment information.

## Do I have questions while using the app?

The application will include a virtual assistant (help bot) that will
guide you step by step through each section, so you're never left
wondering how to use a feature.
